ABOUTElham Fazli · Wedding Photographer & Filmmaker

We may have more in common than you think. We are drawn to beauty, emotion, legacy, and the kind of moments that only happen once. Something timeless, honest, and deeply felt.

I began my journey with a camera because I was never interested in simply recording what happened. I wanted to preserve what it felt like. The quiet nerves before the ceremony, the way families hold each other, the energy on the dance floor, and the small details that tell the full story.

Your Portrait Here

Over the years, I've had the honor of documenting weddings across the country, creating films and photographs for couples who care deeply about their story. My work blends cinematic direction with documentary honesty — so your wedding does not feel staged, but still looks intentional, elegant, and timeless.

Based in Nebraska and available for travel, I create wedding photography and films for couples who want their day captured with care, emotion, and a luxury editorial eye.
